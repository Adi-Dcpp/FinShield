# FinShield Pitch Deck (12 Slides)
## Futuristic AI Cybersecurity Theme Guide

## Master Design System (Apply to Every Slide)
- Background: deep dark purple-blue gradient (`#09041A` -> `#121B3F` -> `#0B1C2F`)
- Accent colors: neon cyan (`#00E5FF`) and neon magenta (`#FF3CAC`)
- Supporting tones: electric blue (`#4D7CFE`), soft glass white (`rgba(255,255,255,0.14)`)
- Typography: clean modern sans-serif (use one family consistently, such as `Sora`, `Manrope`, or `Space Grotesk`)
- Layout rule: left visual zone + right content zone where applicable
- Card style: glassmorphism panels with blur, soft border glow, and high contrast text
- Visual language: subtle circuit lines, network nodes, and light grain textures (very low opacity)
- Motion: subtle fade-in and glow pulse only; no random 3D assets
- Style consistency: no off-theme colors, no mismatched fonts, no unrelated icons

## Global Slide Components
- Header zone: slide title in white with cyan underline accent
- Content zone: glass card(s) with concise bullet points
- Highlight strip: magenta-cyan gradient pill for key quote or takeaway
- Footer: `FinShield | AI-Powered Fraud Prevention`
